Wormhole types and ship restrictions

Every wormhole has a code (shown when you look at it) that says where it leads, how heavy a ship can jump through it, how much mass it takes before it collapses, and how long it lasts. A K162 is the far side of a hole someone opened from elsewhere; it takes the stats of the hole on the other side. All figures are from CCP's data and checked against the live server.

Can my ship go through?

Size limits

The heaviest ship that can jump is set by the hole's per-jump limit. This is base hull mass: propulsion modules, armour plates, Higgs anchor rigs and cargo add more. Titans and supercarriers can't use wormholes at all, whatever their mass.

Reading a wormhole

Show Info is the final word. A wormhole's info window gives the kind of space it leads to as a band (for example “Class 1-3 wormhole systems”, “Low-security systems” or “Drifter wormhole systems”), the largest ship size, the lifetime left (“Less than 1 day” down to “Expired, closure imminent”, the earliest it can close) and the mass left (“More than 50%”, “Less than 50%” or “Less than 10% remaining”). CCP simplified this wording in September 2025; older guides quote the earlier sentences such as “deadly unknown parts of space” for C6.CCP 23.01EVE Uni See the first steps on the overview for both versions. Because Show Info only gives a band of classes, the look of the hole helps pin down the exact class, especially from the K162 side.Ashy 2020

The halo shows the size limit

The coloured aura around the outside of the hole. Every source that covers it agrees, so this is the most reliable visual clue. Sizes match the jump limits in the size limits table above. Labelled in-game photos on the EVE Geological Survey's wormhole page show the same: a blue frigate hole (Q003), a teal medium hole (V301), a grey large hole (B274) and an amber-gold extra-large hole (Z142).EVE Geological Survey

Ageing and mass

A hole's halo moves more and more as it gets close to collapsing: seen in game, and also reported by EVE University, which adds that it looks faded in its last four hours.EVE Uni The hole also shrinks as mass goes through it, most noticeably when it drops to half and to a tenth (player reports). Check Show Info before trusting a hole with an expensive ship.

Rolling a hole

Every jump takes the ship's mass off the hole's total; once the total is used up the hole collapses. Rolling a hole means collapsing it on purpose, by jumping heavy ships back and forth through it.EVE Uni WC

  • To close the door before you work. Every open connection is a way in for hunters. Groups that live in J-space roll the holes they aren't using before they run sites or mine, so they only have to watch for new signatures.EVE forumsBrave 2020–2023
  • To get a new connection. A static comes back as soon as it collapses, leading to a different system of the same kind, so a group can roll its static until it leads somewhere useful: a better route out to k-space, a system with sites to run, or someone to fight.EVE Uni WCEVE forums
  • The risks. Rolling takes heavy ships and time, since each pilot can only jump each way once every five minutes, and the last jumps are a gamble because a hole's true mass varies by up to 10%. Whoever jumps last can be left on the wrong side, so rolling ships carry probes or a cloak, and a hole already at its last tenth is usually finished with a heavy interdictor running a Zero-Point Mass Entangler, which weighs less than a frigate.EVE Uni WC

How much mass each fitting adds:

FittingMass added
  • A propulsion module adds its mass only while it's running; armour plates add theirs all the time. A Higgs Anchor rig doubles the ship's mass.CCP data
  • Show Info reports mass in three steps: more than half left, less than half, and less than a tenth.EVE Uni A hole's total mass isn't exact: each one spawns with 90–110% of the figure listed for its type, so the last jumps of a roll are never certain.EVE Uni
  • A pilot can't jump the same way through the same hole twice within five minutes (polarisation); it follows the pilot, not the ship, which sets the pace of a roll.EVE UniAshy 2021
  • K162s are the far ends of holes opened from elsewhere. One appears on the far side once someone starts a warp to the hole, and that also starts the hole's lifetime clock; until then a fresh hole can sit unseen from the other side.EVE Uni
