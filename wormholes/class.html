Wormhole class

Where its statics lead

Every system has one or more static wormholes that always lead to the same kind of space; a new one appears when the old one dies. Wandering wormholes and K162s (the far side of a hole opened from elsewhere) come on top. Statics are community-recorded: confirmed means Tripwire and Wanderer agree.
