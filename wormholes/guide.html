Wormhole site guide

How the danger tiers work

The tiers are this site's own assessment, not a game value. They rate how hard the site's NPCs are on a pilot who is ready for them; other players are a separate risk everywhere. The rules:

  • Combat anomalies follow their class: C1 and C2 1, C3 2, C4 3, C5 4, C6 5. Expected incoming damage roughly doubles from C1 to C2 and again to C3.Ashy 2020
  • Sleeper data and relic sites rate one tier higher than the anomalies of their class: they are Sleeper combat sites with hacking cans, about as hard as the next class up. The unguarded pirate ones in C1–C3 (below) have no NPCs.Ashy
  • Gas sites are safe until Sleepers arrive 15–20 minutes after the first ship warps in, then as dangerous as the class's waves: Perimeter 1, Frontier 3, Core 4.EVE Uni
  • Ore sites are rated by their defenders' combined damage on the same scale the anomalies roughly follow (under 500 DPS 1, under 900 2, under 1,700 3), one tier higher when a defender can scram, because a mining barge then can't warp out.
  • Bringing a capital into a C5 or C6 site makes it 5, whatever its base tier: extra Sleeper battleships warp in.EVE Uni
  • Labels: 1 Low, 2 Moderate, 3 High, 4 Severe, 5 Extreme.

Sleeper combat sites, data sites and relic sites

Each class from C1 to C6 has four combat anomalies (they show on the probe scanner without scanning) and two data and two relic sites (which have to be scanned down). There are no DED complexes or gated rooms in wormhole space.EVE Uni

How Sleepers fight

  • Sleepers have equal resistance to every damage type, so any weapon works. They deal EM and thermal (beams) and explosive and kinetic (missiles): tank all four.EVE Uni
  • C1–C3 sites were rebuilt in September 2026. Their NPCs now split damage and electronic warfare across pilots, no longer attack drones, capsules or Zephyrs, no longer respawn, and can be neuted. Older advice to pull drones or spread threat in these classes no longer applies.CCP 24.01CCP
  • C4–C6 Sleepers still use the older behaviour: they switch to whoever builds the most threat, go after drones unless you have built threat first, repair each other in higher classes, and neut from about 40 km. The patch notes describe the 2026 changes for C1–C3 only.EVE Uni
  • From C3 up they scram, which stops micro-jump drive escapes.EVE Uni
  • Waves: kill everything else first, then the wave's trigger NPC to bring in the next wave.Ashy
  • Capital escalation (C5 and C6): warping a capital into a combat, data or relic site brings in Upgraded Avenger battleships that web, scram and neut hard. Each capital type can trigger at most two escalations. Since January 2025 a site can be escalated any time before its second wave, and Upgraded Avengers appear only in escalated sites; their loot was raised from 35m to 50m ISK in 2025.EVE UniCCP 22.02CCP 23.01 How many Avengers each capital brings is disputed between sources and not shown until checked.
  • After a C5 or C6 site: a Decloaked Transmission Relay can appear. Attacking it with a capital on grid brings an Arithmos Tyrannos Drifter (required since January 2025); without one, a weaker Hyleus Tyrannos.EVE UniEVE Uni

Drifters

  • Five Drifter systems (Sentinel MZ, Liberated Barbican, Sanctified Vidette, Conflux Eyrie and Azdaja Redoubt) each have six statics into C1–C6. Their k-space entrances, "Unidentified Wormholes" in Jove Observatory systems, have had to be scanned down since March 2026 and are guarded by roaming Drifter battleships.EVE UniCCP 23.02
  • Since the 2025 Drifter crisis NPC factions hold the systems and the old hive sites were reconfigured. Current hive contents and loot aren't confirmed.EVE Uni
  • Drifter battleships carry a doomsday that is limited by tracking and signature radius, which fleets exploit by staying close. Their stats have changed several times. Danger 5.EVE Uni
  • The Drifter Response Battleship that can follow a C5 or C6 site has no EM resistance on its armour (it's 50% against the other types).CCP data Brave's site-running guide recommends thermal damage for its shield and EM for its armour, and at least five points of warp disruption to hold it.Brave 2021
  • Two kinds of Drifter come from a C5 or C6 site. With a capital on grid before the second wave (and kept there until it spawns), damaging the Transmission Relay brings the Drifter Response Battleship: about 2.15 million HP, 1,600 DPS and about 300m ISK of loot. Without one, it brings the smaller Drifter Recon Battleship: 720,000 HP, 1,067 DPS and about 100m ISK. Both scram with 20 points and neut hard; stats in the Sleeper table above.RykkiCCP data
  • Groups that fight them have used Confessors with Guardian or support-cruiser logistics.EVE Uni

Rampant Drone Fabricator

A permanent wormhole-only site in C1–C6 since July 2026: waves of rogue drones that grow with a score based on the ships present, up to 100 waves. You keep your loot when you leave. CCP says it is meant for small teams and that solo pilots rarely get past about wave 40. Danger 2 in the first waves, rising to 5.CCP

Finding a fit

This site doesn't list fittings. EVE Workbench has thousands posted by players, searchable by ship, but anyone can post one and many are old, so check a fit before you buy it. Load it into Pyfa (a free fitting tool) or the in-game fitting window, then hold it up against the numbers in the site tables:

Check what else a fit assumes. Many posted fits include implants and drugs (combat boosters) on top of the ship and modules. These can add a lot to the real price, and some fits only work as shown with them: a tank that is cap-stable or holds a wave only with a particular implant set or booster may fall short without it. Look at the implant and booster list before you buy, and check the fit in Pyfa with and without them.
  1. Can it tank the worst wave? Compare the fit's repair per second (or its EHP, for a buffer fit) with the site's strongest-wave DPS. That figure assumes every ship in the wave shoots you, so it's the worst case; allow for extra spawns (the second figure) and for your system's effect, which can cut your resists or repairs. Sleepers deal all four damage types, so look at your lowest resist, not your best.
  2. How long will it take? The site's total EHP divided by your DPS is the shortest possible clear time: a 300k EHP site at 600 DPS is over eight minutes of shooting before travel and targeting. Big guns do less to the small, fast Sleepers.
  3. Does it survive neuts and scrams? The Sleeper table shows which ships neutralise your capacitor or stop you warping. An active tank that runs dry under neuts will fail however strong it looks on paper.

Speed and size are a tank too. Besides soaking damage with hit points and repairs, you can avoid it. A missile does less damage to a target smaller than its explosion and moving faster than the explosion spreads, and turrets struggle to hit small ships moving fast across them.EVE UniEVE Uni Sleeper battleships are built for big targets: their missiles explode 330 m wide but spread at only 69 m/s, and their turrets track slowly. By the standard missile formula, a cruiser with a 130 m signature at 400 m/s takes about a tenth of a Sleeper battleship's missile damage, while a battleship sitting still takes all of it. Sleeper frigates are the opposite: 40 m explosions spreading at 170 m/s, which hit small ships well (a frigate at 400 m/s still takes about half), turrets that track several times faster than a battleship's, and speeds of 1,750–1,925 m/s, so you can't simply outrun them.CCP data

The catch is webs and scrams. Many Sleepers, frigates included, web for 60%; the Sleeper table shows which. Slowed down, that same cruiser takes about a fifth of the damage instead of a tenth, and its turret protection mostly goes. Several Sleeper scramblers also switch off microwarpdrives.CCP data That, and the way a microwarpdrive makes your signature much bigger, is why players speed-tanking Sleepers use an afterburner, and keep their distance from the webbers.EVE forumsEVE forums player reports, 2014–2020

Where it works. Players report it working best in low classes: a frigate or small cruiser with an afterburner can sig- and speed-tank most C1 spawns, as long as it carries enough buffer or repair for the hits that still land and avoids the sites with heavy neuts or webs.EVE forums It's also how gas harvesters survive once the Sleepers arrive: a Venture or Prospect with an afterburner and a shield booster can keep harvesting rather than fight.EVE forums player reports, 2018 Higher up, more Sleepers web, scram and neut and the damage climbs past 2,000 DPS in C5 gas sites, so speed and signature help a real tank rather than replace it.

Unguarded data and relic sites (C1–C3)

Not every hacking site in a wormhole is guarded. Alongside the Sleeper data and relic sites, C1–C3 systems (and shattered systems) get the ordinary pirate sites also found in null-sec: Ruined … relic sites and Central … data sites, named after a pirate faction (Angel, Blood Raider, Guristas, Sansha or Serpentis). They have no NPCs, no timers and nothing that can go off, so a lone hacking frigate can clear them.Signal CartelEVE Uni Safe from NPCs is not safe from players: someone may have seen your probes.Signal Cartel The name shows at 75% scan strength, so you know whether a site is safe before you warp in.Signal Cartel

Gas

Nine gas sites hold fullerite gases. Sleepers arrive 15–20 minutes after the first ship warps in, whoever it was. The Venture is the standard ship (a harvesting bonus, warp core stabilisers, cheap); the Prospect can fit a covert ops cloak but costs more.EVE Uni Since Fractured Skies (November 2026), the extra components for several Synchronizer Rigs come mainly from C1–C3 content such as Sleeper salvage and reactions from low-class gas.CCP

Ore and ice

  • Perimeter and Frontier deposits appear in every class; Core deposits mainly in C5 and C6, and the two shattered fields only in shattered systems.EVE Uni
  • The asteroids are IV-grade, the highest-yield version of each ore. A 2026 patch summary reports the change and Rykki's Guide switched its tables to IV-grade ores in October 2026;eveapexRykki not yet found in CCP's own notes.
  • Ice exists only in shattered systems: each has one Shattered Ice Field with every type of ice, including the high-yield isotope ices.EVE Uni
  • Every ore site has Sleeper defenders. The Exceptional and Rarified Core Deposits have a Sleepless Sentinel that webs, scrams and neuts, so a mining barge there can't warp away once caught.EVE UniCCP data
  • Moon mining pays like high-sec, not like null. Wormhole moons only ever hold the lowest tier of moon ore, R4 (“Ubiquitous”: Bitumens, Coesite, Sylvite, Zeolites), the same as high-sec; the rarer R8–R64 ores are found only in low- and null-sec.EVE Uni At Jita, R4 ore has averaged about 60 ISK a m³ over the last 30 days, against roughly 240–570 for the higher tiers and 80–280 for the asteroid ores in wormhole ore sites.CCP ESI Prices checked 22 Nov 2026; raw moon ore trades thinly, so treat these as a rough guide. The Metenox Moon Drill (Equinox, 2024) can be anchored on a wormhole moon and extracts on its own, more slowly than a refinery and a mining fleet, but it needs fuel blocks and magmatic gas to run, which eats into an R4 moon's small income.EVE UniCCP

Planets (PI)

Wormhole planets are as rich as null-sec ones and see little competition, which makes planetary interaction one of the most passive incomes in J-space.Ashy 2019 Other players can still deplete a planet, and gas planets are large, so links between structures cost more.Ashy Customs offices in wormholes are player-owned, and the owner sets the tax. Picking up from a customs office means sitting uncloaked next to it, a known place to catch haulers, and everything you make still has to leave through a wormhole.EVE Uni PI isn't possible in shattered systems: their planets yield nothing.EVE Uni

Staying alive

  • D-scan is your early warning. Local chat only shows other pilots once they speak, so run the directional scanner every few seconds: it shows ships, probes and wrecks within 14.4 AU, but not cloaked ships. Combat probes mean act now, not be careful: people who live or hunt in wormholes scan fast, so by the time probes appear at the edge of d-scan you may already be found. Align out, warp off or cloak straight away.EVE UniEVE Uni
  • No probes doesn't mean no one is coming. Combat anomalies show up for everyone without scanning, so a hunter can warp straight to yours without ever launching a probe.EVE Uni Hunters who know a system often bookmark its sites in advance, including spots off to one side ("perches") where they can sit cloaked and watch, or warp in at just the right distance, so the first sign can be a ship landing on top of you.EVE UniEVE forums player knowledge
  • Watch for new signatures. Keep the probe scanner open and treat any new signature as a threat until someone has checked it: a K162 means someone has just opened a hole into your system, while a freshly spawned wandering hole is less urgent. Groups that rat safely stop and align out when a signature appears, keep scouts on their holes, and roll connections they aren't using.EVE forumsBrave 2020–2023
  • Fit to slip away. A ship that aligns in 2 seconds or less from a standstill enters warp before most attackers can lock it (align times round up to whole server ticks, so anything under 2.00 s counts). Inertial stabilisers, nanofiber structures, agility rigs and implants all help; the fitting window shows your align time. Keep aligned to a safe spot or a hole while you work. Warp core stabilisers rarely beat a determined tackler.EVE Uni Inertial stabilisers make your signature bigger, which trades against the next point.EVE forums
  • Be hard to probe. How easily combat probes find you depends on your signature radius against your sensor strength: a smaller signature and stronger sensors take longer to pin down. Sensor boosters with ECCM scripts, signal amplifiers and faction sensor-strength implants raise sensor strength. Player tests find the benefit stops at a sensor strength of about your signature radius divided by 1.1, so pushing it higher adds nothing. Nothing is unprobeable any more: this only buys time, and a scanner in a scanning-bonused ship with the Virtue implant set (which boosts probe strength) will always find you.EVE forumsEVE forums player tests, 2021–2022 It doesn't help in a combat anomaly, which needs no scanning. Only a cloaked ship can't be probed.EVE Uni
  • Check your connections before you start: scout them, and look for structures with pilots docked.Krabbi
  • Expect to lose ships eventually, and keep what you fly affordable.Ashy
  • Structures in wormholes have no asset safety.Ashy 2021
