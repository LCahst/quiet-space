Wormhole space at a glance

Wormhole space (J-space) is 2,604 unmapped systems reached only through wormholes, with no stargates and a delayed local channel. Each system has a class that sets how hard its Sleeper sites are and which wormholes it connects to. Pick a class for its effects, statics and sites, or look up a single system. The site guide covers what you can do there and what to fly, and hole types what fits through each wormhole.

Learn d-scan first. If you're thinking about flying in J-space and you haven't learned how to use the directional scanner (d-scan), now is the time. Local chat only shows other pilots once they speak, so d-scan is your early warning: run it every few seconds to see the ships, probes and wrecks within 14.4 AU of you, and if a ship you don't recognise turns up, leave or cloak. It can't see cloaked ships. Combat probes mean act now, not be careful. People who live or hunt in wormholes get very good at scanning, and a practised prober can pin you down in seconds, so by the time probes show at the edge of your d-scan you may already be found: align out, warp off or cloak straight away.EVE Uni More in Staying alive.

Just scanned down a wormhole?

  1. Bookmark it the moment you arrive. The game doesn't remember where you came in; without a bookmark you have to scan your way out again. Carry a probe launcher and probes in anything you take through.EVE Uni
  2. Show Info on the hole (right-click it). It shows four lines; a K162 shows its destination too.

  3. Look up the code. Anything other than K162 has a fixed destination, size, mass and lifetime: see hole types. A K162 is the far end of a hole someone opened from the other side.EVE Uni
  4. Know the jump rules. You can't jump the same way through the same hole twice within five minutes (polarisation, which follows the pilot, not the ship). Weapon timers don't stop you jumping.EVE Uni
  5. Have a way out. If your hole collapses you need another exit: a static, which respawns when it dies, or probes to find one. Stranded without probes, ask in the in-game EvE-Scout channel: Signal Cartel's rescue service keeps password-locked caches anchored across wormhole space, each with a probe launcher and scanner probes, and a scout will give you the location and password of one near you, or send a search-and-rescue team if there isn't one.PC GamerEVE UniEvE-Scout Filaments won't get you out: travel filaments (Needlejack, Pochven and the rest) can't be activated inside a wormhole system, so the only ways out of J-space are a wormhole you scan down or a route someone has already found.
  6. Map your chain. Wormhole connections change every day, and nothing in the game remembers them for you. A mapper is a website where you, or your whole group, record each hole as you scan it: where it leads, its code, how old it is and how much mass is left. That gives everyone the same picture of the chain (the string of connected systems), the shortest way out to k-space, and which holes are about to die; most sign you in with your EVE account and can follow where you are as you jump. Current ones include Wanderer, WormholeSystems and Wayfinder, which took over from the public Pathfinder server in 2026. Mappers come and go, so CCP's list of community tools is a good place to look; CCP lists them but doesn't endorse them. For getting across New Eden rather than living in J-space, EVE Metro is a paid service, 50 million ISK a month paid in game, that keeps shared bookmarks of wormhole and Thera shortcuts and plans routes through them.EVE Metro price as listed Nov 2026EVE UniEVE Uni

Kinds of wormhole space

Most systems are ordinary C1–C6 wormholes, but several kinds play quite differently. Counts below come from CCP's data.

Classes

Found a system with an effect?

About four in ten C1–C6 systems have a star that changes every ship in the system, yours and everyone else's, Sleepers included. The system info panel tells you which one. Every effect has upsides and downsides, and gets stronger with the class.CCP data

Your system's class

Numbers are CCP's, at the strength for the class you pick. Green helps you, red hurts you, white cuts both ways. Look up a single system on system search for its exact effect.
