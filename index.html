Traffic comes from ESI’s public /universe/system_jumps/ and /universe/system_kills/ routes, which CCP updates once an hour. ESI doesn’t publish player counts per system, so gate jumps in the last hour are the closest stand-in for population. NPC kills usually mean someone is ratting there.

Systems missing from ESI’s jump list had zero jumps. Wormhole space isn’t included, because ESI only counts stargate jumps. System names and security are cached in your browser for 30 days after the first load.

Station markers cover NPC stations only. On the hosted site, public player structures come from EVE Ref’s daily structure data; private ones can’t be seen by anyone outside their owners. Installing a jump clone at an NPC station may need standings with the station’s owner.

On the hosted site, map data, 30-day averages, weekly patterns and system sizes are prepared by a scheduled job, so they load instantly. Opened as a local file, the page fetches what it needs from ESI instead.

Pirate insurgency warnings come from CCP’s Frontlines data, checked hourly on the hosted site. It isn’t an official API, so warnings disappear rather than go stale if that data stops updating.

In Loud mode, ISK destroyed and each system’s biggest kills come from zKillboard’s live kill feed, collected hourly on the hosted site.

Ice belt systems come from the EVE University wiki, since ESI doesn’t list ice. That list covers high and low sec, but is still missing Caldari and Minmatar low-sec ice systems and all null-sec ice.
