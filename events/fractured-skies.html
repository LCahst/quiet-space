Fractured Skies: Military Campaigns

Each empire runs a campaign to find the Emergent Artifact, raced against its rival: Amarr against Minmatar, Caldari against Gallente. You help by running expedition sites, hacking caches or taking intel from faction warfare complexes, then trading what you find to your empire's trader for ISK, loyalty points and campaign tokens. You don't have to be enlisted in faction warfare.

Before you hand anything in: open the objective in the Opportunities window and click Participate now. Without it you still get the token, but no ISK and no LP for the items.

Pick your empire

Campaign progress changes by the hour, so we don't copy it here: check it in game under Theaters of War, Campaigns, or on each objective in the Opportunities window.

What to do

Three objectives are open in every campaign. The mining objectives are already finished in all four, so mining no longer earns ISK or LP; the ore can still be traded for tokens.

Turning in

    The LP is paid per 100 items, so a 450-item trade leaves part of an LP block unpaid. Trading in multiples of 900 (two tokens at a time) loses nothing. The LP for expedition and cache items goes to the empire's navy; for intel, to its faction warfare militia.

    Turn in or sell?

    What each item is worth if you sell it at Jita now, against what turning it in pays: the ISK and LP for the item plus a 450th of a token at its Jita price. Set what an LP is worth to you; 800 ISK is a cautious figure for empire navy LP.

    Trader shop

    ISK per token uses the Jita buy price (what you'd get selling now). Items marked "rarely traded" changed hands fewer than 10 times in 30 days, so their order book says little about their worth. The scanning blueprint crates can't be sold on the market.

      Also running

      SARO Black Sites

      High-sec and low-sec, spawned by EVE Vanguard players. Find them on the map with the Activity filter set to SARO Black Sites.

      SARO Lockup (combat)
      High-sec: three waves and a boss; only the boss drops loot. Low-sec: two waves overlapping the boss, harder with better loot; don't use drones there, as the boss has a Vorton Projector. Players use a Zealot or Vagabond. Also drops a few expedition items.
      SARO Surveillance Outpost (data, level 2)
      Two cans to hack; also drops a few cache items.
      SARO Ore Holding (mining)
      Campaign ore for all four empires: X-Grade in high-sec, Z-Grade in low-sec.

      Rogue Drone Outbreaks

      Mostly in Gallente space. Find them in the Opportunities window under Interstellar Events.

      Infested Research Facility (combat)
      No ship restrictions. Three waves up to battlecruisers; kill the jammers first, then shoot the Infestation Rendezvous Cluster for the loot. Deal and tank EM and thermal.
      Infestation Harborage (data, level 2)
      Three cans to hack.
      Loot
      Outbreak Initiation Thread, sold to NPC buy orders for about 100k ISK each; roughly 10m ISK per site.
