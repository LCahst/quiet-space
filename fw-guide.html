Which complexes can my ship enter?

Faction warfare is fought over complexes: combat sites you capture by staying inside them. Most complexes have an entry gate that only lets certain ships through, so the ship you bring decides where you can fight. Pick your ship below to see where it fits.

Reading complex names on the probe scanner

Complexes show up as combat sites in your probe scanner, and their names tell you everything you need before you warp. Take this one:

Gallentewhose side the complex belongs to
Smallthe size, which sets the biggest hull allowed
ADVthe tier, which sets which kinds of ship are allowed
-1how many pilots the reward scales to

That last number matters for your wallet. The loyalty point (LP) reward for capturing a site is shared among the pilots in it, and it scales up to that many pilots. Bring more than the number shown and each pilot’s LP payout starts to shrink. A “-1” site is built for one pilot; a “-4” site can reward four without anyone losing out.

The first time anyone warps to a complex, it appears on everyone’s overview in that system, so you can warp straight to it after that. You can also spot ships sitting in an active complex with a narrow, short-range directional scan (combat recon ships are the exception: they don’t show up this way).

The four tiers, from strictest to most open

Basic (BSC)
Tech 1 ships only.
Navy (NVY)
Tech 1 and navy ships.
Advanced (ADV)
Tech 1, navy, pirate and Tech 2 ships.
Elite (ELT)
Everything, Tech 3 included. Tech 3 destroyers get +500 shield, armor and hull inside.

Open complexes sit outside this system entirely: there’s no gate at all, so any ship can fly in, from a frigate all the way up to a titan.

How long it takes to capture

Small complexes are quick and big ones take longer. The smallest frigate-only scout sites with a basic gate take about 5 minutes; the other scout, assault frigate and small sites take about 10; medium, moderate, large and open sites take about 15. Plan for the whole timer: you have to stay inside the capture area until it runs out.

Rewards: victory points and loyalty points

Every site you capture does two things. It moves the system toward changing hands, measured in victory points (VP): it takes 75,000 VP to make an enemy system vulnerable, and capturing sites in your own system takes VP away from the attacker. And it pays you loyalty points (LP), the militia currency you spend in its LP store.

How reliable are these numbers? Faction warfare has changed many times, and no single source has everything. We cross-checked CCP’s own patch notes, the EVE University Wiki and eve-fw.net. Victory point values marked C come straight from CCP. The LP figures agree between the wiki and eve-fw.net, but the wiki notes its LP table may be out of date, so treat exact payouts as a guide and check in game.

How the LP works

  • Where you plex matters most. Frontline systems pay 50% more than command operations systems, and rearguard systems pay almost nothing (a 99% cut).
  • LP is split, not earned per minute. Everyone inside the capture radius when the timer finishes gets an equal share, however long they were there. Leave early and you get nothing.
  • Defending pays only when it’s needed. Capturing a site in your own faction’s system pays the full reward while that system is contested or vulnerable, and nothing once it’s stable.
  • Battlefields are the big ones. A won battlefield gives 2,000 VP and 150,000 LP to the winning side, shared fully by up to 30 pilots.

The exceptions that catch people out

  • Tech 3 ships need a bigger gate than their size suggests. A Tech 3 destroyer can’t use a Small advanced complex, but fits into a Medium advanced one. A Tech 3 cruiser can’t use a Medium advanced complex, but fits into a Large advanced one. The one special case is an elite site built for Tech 3 destroyers, the Contested Field Research Facility.
  • Some ships count as pirate ships, even if they don’t look it. Sisters of EVE ships (Astero, Stratios, Nestor), Society of Conscious Thought ships (Metamorphosis, Sunesis, Gnosis, Praxis), EDENCOM ships (Skybreaker, Stormbringer, Thunderchild) and Triglavian ships (Damavik, Vedmak, Drekavac, Leshak) are all treated as pirate ships, so they need an advanced or elite gate.
  • Assault frigates have their own site. Assault frigate complexes let only assault frigates in, which makes them a good place for an even fight.
  • Some sites reward the right hull. Elite sites give Tech 3 destroyers an extra 500 shield, armor and hull, and Moderate sites boost battlecruiser skill bonuses by 20% (Command Ship skills excepted), so those are the places to bring them.
  • Moderate advanced sites only appear during pirate insurgencies. Normal faction warfare has the Moderate navy site; the advanced version spawns in insurgency systems from stage 2.

Pirate insurgencies

  • While pirates are running an insurgency in a system, every complex there is an advanced one, so more kinds of ship can get in.
  • If a system reaches the highest corruption level (stage 5), low-sec starts to behave much like null-sec, warp bubbles included. Fly with that in mind.
  • You can follow live insurgencies, corruption and suppression on our faction warfare map with the pirate focus button.

Flying in without joining a militia

You don’t have to join a militia to take part, but entering a complex as a neutral marks you as a suspect. That means militia pilots can attack you without any security status penalty, while you still take the usual security status and standing hits if you attack first.
